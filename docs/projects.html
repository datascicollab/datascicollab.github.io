Active Projects

AnVIL

The NHGRI AnVIL (Genomic Data Science Analysis, Visualization, and Informatics Lab-space) is a project powered by Terra for biomedical researchers to access data, run analysis tools, and collaborate. The AnVIL platform is useful for researchers, clinicians, and educators. Given the multifaceted offerings of the AnVIL platform, we support users with a variety of resources, hosting events, and soliciting feedback from the AnVIL community.

Baltimore Community Data Science

This course was developed in partnership with SOURCE at the Johns Hopkins Bloomberg School of Public Health, the community engagement and service learning center for Johns Hopkins schools of Public Health, Nursing, and Medicine. We partner with community-based organizations (CBOs) to collaboratively work on data science projects. Students learn service learning skills as well as data science skills.

Bio-OCS

The Open Case Studies project is an educational resource of experiential guides that demonstrate how to effectively derive knowledge from data in real-world challenges. Resources are intended for educators, students, and independent learners. The original OCS provides case studies focused on data science skills for public health topics.

Bio-OCS is a new phase of OCS, providing case studies focused on biomedical topics and building bioinformatics skills using cutting-edge methodologies and tools.

DaSEH

Data Science for Environmental Health (DaSEH) is an NIEHS-funded short course that combines online learning and an in-person project-focused intensive. DaSEH is tailored for beginners and novices in R programming, offering instruction on importing, wrangling, visualizing, and analyzing data. It provides hands-on training in using R for statistical computing, a widely-used open-source tool for data analysis and visualization.

GEMs

The Genomics Education Modules (GEMs) Program is a collaborative project between Fred Hutch Cancer Center, Clovis Community College, Guttman Community College, El Paso Community College, and Georgetown University. It focuses on creating educational materials and instructor training for genomic data science, specifically at two-year and teaching intensive colleges.

Genomic Data Science Community Network / BioDIGS

The Genomic Data Science Community Network (GDSCN) is a faculty network that works towards a vision where researchers, educators, and students from all backgrounds are able to fully participate in genomic data science research. The GDSCN research consortium is the BioDIGS (BioDiversity and Informatics for Genomics Scholars) Project, which aims to be at the intersection of genomics, data science, cloud computing, and education. Students and faculty are equipped to explore local soil microbiomes using both short- and long-read metagenomic sequencing.

Introduction to R for Public Health Researchers

This course has been taught by our team for several years at the Johns Hopkins Bloomberg School of Public Health during the summer and winter intersession program and has been rated as excellent by the students. The course introduces R and principles of data science for students and practitioners of public health. The content includes data import, data wrangling, statistical analysis, data visualization, data export, functions and more. It is intended for those with little to no R experience. The structure includes lectures and hands-on labs. Recordings of previous iterations of the course are available on our website, as well as all of the other materials.

ITCR Training Network

Informatics Technology for Cancer Research (ITCR) Training Network (ITN) is a collaborative effort of researchers around the United States to support cancer informatics and data science training through resources, technology, and events. The mission of ITN is to catalyze cancer informatics research through training opportunities. A significant aim is to bridge the gap between ITCR tool developers and the users of those tools by promoting tool usability and strengthening user preparedness. Resources are available across several modalities (courses, cheatsheets, videos, publications, etc.) and are intended for combinations of researchers, software developers, and leaders.

Tidyverse Course

This course introduces a powerful set of data science tools known as the Tidyverse. This course on the Tidyverse ecosystem of R packages cover different aspects of the data science pipeline, from importing the data, tidying the data into a format that is easy to work with, exploring and visualizing the data, and fitting machine learning models.
